For You, From Friends Turning TikTok's inbox into a human-curated, taste-graph feed

1 · TL;DR

TikTok mastered the interest graph (what people like you watch) and ignored the taste graph (what your specific friends know you would love). The most valuable, least algorithm-replicable signal on the platform, a friend deliberately picking a video and saying "this is so you," is thrown away the instant it happens.

For You, From Friends transforms the inbox into what it's actually used for. Today the inbox is a chat UI wrapped around a behavior that isn't chatting; it's mostly videos friends sent you. We split it into From Friends (a swipeable feed of everything friends hand-picked for you, tagged by sender) and Chats (real conversations). One positive gesture, swipe right = "this is so me," sends warm, private recognition back to the curator. It gives the roughly half of users who never post a real creative identity: the curator.

2 · Problem & background

The behavioral truth. Most users are not creators. Only about 52% of US adult TikTok users have ever posted a video; 98% of public videos come from the top 25% most active (Pew, 2024). The dominant creative act for everyone else is the send.

The gap. That send is a dead-end. The sender gets no signal their taste landed; the received video is flattened into a message bubble and forgotten.

Why it exists. TikTok's DNA is the algorithmic interest graph, so the human curation layer was never the point, and never designed for.

Why now. Instagram's Adam Mosseri (Dec 2025): "That feed is dead… the primary way people share now is in DMs," and "sends per reach" is a top ranking signal. TikTok shipped Shared Feed (Dec 2025, inside DMs); Instagram shipped Blend (Apr 2025). All of these keep the algorithm as the curator and the human as a passive consumer. That whitespace is the opportunity.

3 · Insight

Interest graph vs. taste graph. The For You page optimizes "what will this person watch." A friend optimizes "what will land for this specific person I know." The metaphor: your friends are matchmakers, and every video they send is a setup.

4 · Goals & non-goals

Goals

  • Give the non-creator majority a durable creative identity (the curator).
  • Turn the one-way send into a closed loop: send, receive, recognize, reciprocate.
  • Grow two metrics the business cares about: sends (a ranking signal) and friend-graph density (a retention driver).

Non-goals

  • Not replacing or reordering the For You page.
  • Not a new bottom-nav tab (that's the graveyard).
  • No public leaderboard, ranking, streak, or negative signal.
  • Not a new algorithmic co-recommendation feed (Shared Feed / Blend already do that).

5 · The core loop

  • Send. A curator picks a video and sends it, optionally with a caption.
  • Receive. It lands in the From Friends feed: full-screen, swipeable, tagged with who sent it.
  • Recognize. Swipe right = "this is so me," a warm private ping to the sender.
  • Reciprocate. One tap to send something back, strengthening the tie.

6 · Architecture & placement (locked)

The feed lives inside the existing Inbox tab as a segmented view. It replaces nothing and adds no nav item.

  • Segmented toggle at the top of the inbox: From Friends | Chats. From Friends is the default view; Chats is the traditional message list, one tap away, with all text messages fully readable.
  • From Friends is a meshed aggregate feed. Each card is tagged, "Milo sent you this · 'this is so you'," with the caption shown when the sender added one.
  • Interaction: swipe up = next; swipe right = "this is so me" (positive only); no negative gesture, ever.
  • Per-chat shared archives are Phase 2.

Why the inbox and not the Home tabs: a swipe-to-match gesture in the main feed fights the For You vertical scroll. TikTok's own Shared Feed already lives inside DMs, so the platform is telling us the friend layer belongs in the messaging surface.

7 · Onboarding, the "What's New" tour

Because the default view changes, a lightweight first-run tour orients the user (3 steps, skippable):

  • "Your inbox is now a feed." Everything friends sent you, in one place.
  • "Swipe right to give credit." They'll get a warm little note. No dislikes, ever.
  • "Switch anytime." Tap Chats for text conversations. Keep sharing great taste and you'll earn an optional Curator badge.

8 · Guardrails, the "private party" principle

The closest analog, Snapchat Streaks, is now linked to documented teen anxiety, and TikTok itself shipped anti-doomscrolling features in Nov 2025. This feature is built to avoid scoring social behavior into pressure.

  • Positive-only. The system only ever notifies you of good news. No negative signal exists to farm anxiety from.
  • Invisible by default. Credit is private between friends. No public counts, no global rank.
  • No streaks, no decay, no loss aversion. Nothing expires to manufacture compulsion.
  • Recognition, not comparison. Status rewards quality of taste, never a ranking against others.
  • Opt-in and reversible at every step.

9 · Roadmap

MVP · now
  • Inbox "From Friends / Chats" toggle (feed default)
  • Meshed swipe feed, sender-tagged, optional captions
  • Swipe-right "this is so me" to a private ping
  • "What's new" quick tour
Phase 2 · next
  • Opt-in Curator badge, earned from distinct friends (un-spammable), comparison-free
  • Group "meme-god," celebratory and rotating
  • Per-chat shared archives; curator identity on profile
Phase 3 · vision
  • Taste-graph flywheel: human "this is so me" labels improve recommendations (framed as better experience; ad relevance is a downstream benefit, gated by privacy guardrails)
  • Creator ecosystem: "send this to someone it reminds you of" CTAs; resonant content reaches smaller creators through trusted human curation

11 · Impact & value

For the user.

The roughly half who never post gain a creative identity as curators. Received shares stop dying. Recognition is warm and pressure-free.

For the creator.

Human curation becomes a high-trust distribution channel. "Send this to someone" gets a real destination. Smaller creators get a discovery path the pure algorithm doesn't offer.

For the business.

Grows sends (a ranking signal) and friend-graph density (a leading retention indicator). The human "this is so me" label is a higher-quality taste signal than watch-time, improving recommendations and, downstream, ad relevance.

Balancing business vs. wellbeing: the engagement gains are designed to come from connection, not manufactured anxiety. Recognition over competition, positive-only over dislikes, invisible-by-default over public scores.

12 · Open questions

  • Naming of the badge (Curator? Taste-maker?) and whether the group meme-god nod is monthly or event-based.
  • Cold-start: seed the feed from existing DM-shared videos for users whose friends aren't sending yet?
  • Group sends: land in each person's meshed feed, a shared group lane, or both?
